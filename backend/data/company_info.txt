NovaPay - Company Handbook Extract

LEAVE AND TIME OFF

Employees receive 20 days of paid leave per calendar year, accrued monthly.
Unused leave carries over to a maximum of 5 days into the following year.
Sick leave is separate and is not capped, but absences beyond three
consecutive days require a medical note.

REMOTE WORK

The standard arrangement is two days remote per week. Engineering teams may
agree a different split with their manager provided core hours of 11:00-16:00
are covered. Fully remote arrangements require director approval.

DEPARTMENTS

Engineering, HR and Finance. Engineering is divided into Platform, Payments
and Reliability.

PAYMENTS PLATFORM

The Payment Service is the system of record for transactions. It was rebuilt
around an append-only event log in 2026 so that settlement and monitoring
could be added without further changes to the write path.

The service persists transactions to PostgreSQL 16 following DEC-004. The
previous MySQL 8.0 cluster is being decommissioned under PAY-133, with
dual-write in place during the migration window.

AUTHENTICATION

Service-to-service calls use OAuth 2.1 client credentials. Token lifetime is
15 minutes. The previous model, static credentials issued from the platform
vault, was retired under DEC-002.

DATA RETENTION

Transaction records are retained for seven years from the date of settlement
to satisfy financial reporting obligations. Deletion before that period
requires written finance approval.

MONITORING

Real-time transaction monitoring covers failed, delayed and duplicate
transactions. Alerting thresholds are defined in the Transaction Monitoring
Specification.
